# 🎒 Arcadia University — Academic Departments

## 1. Faculty of Computing & AI
- Departments: Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, Data Science
- Head: Dr. Leyla Kenan
- Location: Tech Hub (Building T)
- Popular courses: CS101 Intro to Programming, AI301 Machine Learning,
  DS210 Data Visualization

## 2. Faculty of Natural Sciences
- Departments: Biology, Chemistry, Physics, Environmental Science
- Head: Dr. Marcus Idris
- Location: Science Wing (Building S)
- Labs open Mon–Sat, 8 AM – 9 PM

## 3. Faculty of Arts & Humanities
- Departments: Literature, History, Philosophy, Fine Arts
- Head: Dr. Priya Nair
- Location: Arts Pavilion (Building P)
- Hosts the annual Meridian Arts Festival

## 4. Faculty of Business & Economics
- Departments: Management, Finance, Marketing
- Head: Dr. Samuel Okafor
- Location: Business Center (Building B)
- Runs the Arcadia Startup Incubator

## 5. Faculty of Health Sciences
- Departments: Nursing, Public Health, Kinesiology
- Head: Dr. Lena Hartmann
- Location: Wellness Complex (Building W)
- Partners with Meridian Heights General Hospital

## 6. Advising
- Each student is assigned a faculty advisor.
- Advisor assignments appear on your ALP dashboard.
- Meetings recommended at least twice per semester.

## 7. Departments Quest (Campus Compass)
🎮 **Quest: "Faculty Explorer"**
- Visit one building from each faculty.
- Answer one trivia question per faculty.
- Reward: "Polymath" badge (+100 XP).
