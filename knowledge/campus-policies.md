# 🚨 Arcadia University — Campus Policies

## 1. Code of Conduct
All students must follow the Arcadia Code of Conduct, available in full on
the ALP. Key points:
- No harassment, hazing, or discrimination.
- No alcohol or drugs in academic buildings.
- No unauthorized access to restricted areas.

## 2. Smoking & Vaping
- Prohibited in all indoor spaces and within 25 feet of building entrances.
- Designated smoking areas: Parking Lot C and Riverside Trail entrance.

## 3. Sustainability
- Arcadia is a single-use plastic-free campus.
- Recycling bins are in every building.
- Bikes are encouraged; racks are across campus.

## 4. Parking
- Student permits: $120/semester, apply via parking.arcadia.edu.
- Visitor parking: Lot A, free for 2 hours.
- Fines: $25 (first offense), $50 (repeat).

## 5. Guests & Visitors
- Students may host up to 2 guests overnight with prior registration.
- Guests must carry a visitor pass.

## 6. Academic Policies
- See the Student Handbook for grading and integrity rules.
- See the Registration Guide for add/drop and withdrawal policies.

## 7. Technology & Data
- Use of campus Wi-Fi is subject to the Acceptable Use Policy.
- Do not share ALP credentials.

## 8. Emergency Procedures
- Fire: evacuate immediately, use stairs, assemble at Central Quad.
- Lockdown: follow Campus Security instructions.
- Medical: call ext. 911.
