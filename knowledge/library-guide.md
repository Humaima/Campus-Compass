# 📚 Arcadia University — Library Guide

## 1. The Meridian Library
Located at the center of campus (Building L), the Meridian Library is open to
all students, staff, and alumni.

## 2. Hours
| Day | Hours |
|-----|-------|
| Mon–Thu | 7:00 AM – 12:00 AM |
| Friday | 7:00 AM – 10:00 PM |
| Saturday | 9:00 AM – 8:00 PM |
| Sunday | 10:00 AM – 10:00 PM |
| Exam Week | 24/7 |

## 3. Services
- Book borrowing (up to 10 books, 3 weeks)
- E-journal and database access (JSTOR, IEEE, PubMed)
- Interlibrary loans
- Research consultations (book via ALP)
- Quiet study zones (Floors 3–4)
- Group study rooms (book online, 2-hour slots)

## 4. Printing & Scanning
- B&W printing: $0.05/page
- Color printing: $0.25/page
- Free scanning to your Arcadia email
- Print stations on Floors 1 and 2

## 5. Study Rooms
- 12 group rooms available.
- Reserve via library.arcadia.edu/rooms.
- No-shows forfeit the room after 15 minutes.

## 6. Library Quest (Campus Compass)
🎮 **Quest: "The Lost Manuscript"**
- Find three hidden QR codes inside the library.
- Each unlocks a puzzle clue.
- Solve all three to earn the "Bookworm" badge (+75 XP).

## 7. Contact
- Front Desk: library@arcadia.edu | ext. 4200
