# 📝 Arcadia University — Registration Guide

## 1. Registration Timeline (Fall Semester)
| Phase | Dates | Who |
|-------|-------|-----|
| Priority Registration | Aug 1–7 | Seniors & Grad students |
| Open Registration | Aug 8–20 | All students |
| Late Registration | Aug 21–27 | With $50 fee |
| Add/Drop Deadline | Sep 10 | All students |

## 2. How to Register
1. Log in to the **Arcadia Learning Portal (ALP)** at portal.arcadia.edu.
2. Navigate to *Academics → Course Registration*.
3. Search by course code, department, or instructor/teacher.
4. Add courses to your cart and click **Enroll**.
5. Confirm your schedule and download your timetable.

## 3. Prerequisites
- Some courses require prerequisites (e.g., CS201 requires CS101).
- The system will block enrollment if prerequisites are unmet.
- Contact your academic advisor for waivers.

## 4. Credit Load
- Minimum full-time load: **12 credits**
- Maximum without approval: **18 credits**
- Overload (19+ credits) requires Dean approval.

## 5. Waitlists
- If a course is full, you may join the waitlist.
- You'll be notified by email if a spot opens (48 hours to accept).

## 6. Withdrawals
- Withdrawing before Sep 10 = no penalty (W not recorded).
- Withdrawing after Oct 15 = "W" on transcript.

## 7. Registration Quest (Campus Compass)
🎮 **Quest: "Enroll Like a Pro"**
- Visit the Registrar's Office on the campus map.
- Complete a mock registration in under 3 minutes.
- Reward: +50 XP, "Registered" badge.

## 8. Contact
- Registrar's Office: registrar@arcadia.edu | Room 105, Administration Building
