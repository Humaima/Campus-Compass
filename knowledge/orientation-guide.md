# Arcadia University — Orientation Guide

## 1. Welcome, New Arcadians!
Orientation Week runs the **last week of September**. All first-year and
transfer students are required to attend.

## 2. Orientation Schedule (Sample)
| Day | Activity |
|-----|----------|
| Mon | Check-in, ID pickup, campus tour |
| Tue | Faculty meet-and-greets, advisor meetings |
| Wed | Library & IT workshops |
| Thu | Clubs & societies fair |
| Fri | Welcome ceremony + Campus Compass launch |

## 3. What to Bring
- Government-issued ID
- Laptop and charger
- Comfortable shoes (lots of walking)
- Reusable water bottle

## 4. First-Week Checklist
- [ ] Activate your Arcadia email
- [ ] Log in to ALP
- [ ] Pick up your student ID
- [ ] Meet your academic advisor
- [ ] Join at least one club

## 5. Campus Compass Launch
🎮 **Main Quest: "First Steps"**
- Complete all 5 orientation checkpoints on the map.
- Each checkpoint unlocks a mini-game or trivia card.
- Finish to earn the "Orientation Champion" badge (+150 XP) and
  a $10 Cosmos Café voucher.

## 6. Orientation Leaders
- Look for staff in **blue Arcadia shirts**.
- They can answer questions and guide you to any building.

## 7. Accessibility
- Accessible routes are marked on the Campus Compass map.
- Contact accessibility@arcadia.edu for accommodations.

## 8. Contact
- Orientation Office: orientation@arcadia.edu
