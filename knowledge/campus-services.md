# 🛎️ Arcadia University — Campus Services

## 1. Student Services Center
Location: Building A, Ground Floor
Hours: Mon–Fri, 8:30 AM – 5:30 PM

Services include:
- ID card issuance and replacement
- Transcript requests
- Financial aid inquiries
- Enrollment verification

## 2. ID Cards
- New students receive IDs during Orientation.
- Replacement cost: $20
- Report lost cards immediately to prevent misuse.

## 3. Financial Aid Office
- Room 112, Admin Building
- Email: finaid@arcadia.edu
- Walk-in hours: Tue & Thu, 1–4 PM

## 4. Career Services
- Resume reviews, mock interviews, job fairs
- Location: Building B, Room 305
- Book via careers.arcadia.edu

## 5. IT Helpdesk
- Location: Library, Floor 1
- Email: help@arcadia.edu
- Phone: ext. 4357 (HELP)
- Handles Wi-Fi, ALP login, software installs

## 6. Wellness Center
- Building C
- Free counseling (5 sessions/semester)
- Walk-in nurse: Mon–Fri, 9 AM – 4 PM

## 7. Services Quest (Campus Compass)
🎮 **Quest: "Campus Navigator"**
- Visit 4 service desks (IT, Financial Aid, Wellness, Career).
- Scan each location on the map.
- Reward: "Well-Connected" badge (+60 XP).

## 8. Emergency Contacts
- Campus Security: ext. 911
- Non-emergency: ext. 4000
