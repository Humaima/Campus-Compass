# 🧾 Arcadia University — Campus Facilities

## 1. The Hearth Cafeteria
- Location: Student Union, Ground Floor
- Hours: 7 AM – 10 PM daily
- Stations: Grill, Vegan, International, Dessert
- Meal plans accepted; cash and card also welcome

## 2. Cosmos Café
- Location: Library, Floor 1
- Hours: 7:30 AM – 8 PM
- Known for: espresso, pastries, quiet vibes

## 3. Arcadia Sports Center
- Location: Building G
- Facilities: Olympic pool, gym, indoor courts, climbing wall
- Hours: Mon–Fri 6 AM – 11 PM, Weekends 8 AM – 9 PM
- Free for students with ID; guests $5

## 4. Meridian Auditorium
- Capacity: 1,200 seats
- Hosts: guest lectures, film screenings, concerts
- Ticket office: auditorium.arcadia.edu

## 5. Student Union
- Home to clubs, lounges, and the Game Room
- Game Room: pool tables, arcade, VR stations
- Club fair every September

## 6. Outdoor Spaces
- Central Quad (events, picnics)
- Meridian Gardens (quiet study)
- Riverside Trail (jogging, cycling)

## 7. Facilities Quest (Campus Compass)
🎮 **Quest: "Hidden Hotspots"**
- Find the VR station in the Game Room.
- Take a photo at Meridian Gardens.
- Grab a snack at Cosmos Café.
- Reward: "Campus Local" badge (+80 XP).

## 8. Bookings
- Sports Center: sports.arcadia.edu
- Auditorium: auditorium.arcadia.edu
