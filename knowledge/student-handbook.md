# 🏫 Arcadia University — Student Handbook

## 1. Welcome to Arcadia
Arcadia University is a fictional institution founded in 1987 in the town of Meridian Heights. Our motto is **"Curiosity Leads the Way."** The university serves approximately 8,500 students across five faculties.

## 2. Academic Integrity
All students must uphold the Arcadia Honor Code:
- All submitted work must be your own unless collaboration is explicitly allowed.
- Plagiarism, cheating, and fabrication are grounds for academic penalty.
- Suspected violations are reviewed by the Academic Integrity Board within 10 days.

## 3. Attendance
- Attendance is required for labs, tutorials, and seminars.
- Lectures are recorded and available on the Arcadia Learning Portal (ALP).
- Missing more than 30% of lab sessions may result in a grade penalty.

## 4. Grading Scale
| Grade | Percentage | GPA |
|-------|-----------|-----|
| A     | 90–100    | 4.0 |
| B     | 80–89     | 3.0 |
| C     | 70–79     | 2.0 |
| D     | 60–69     | 1.0 |
| F     | Below 60  | 0.0 |

## 5. Student Conduct
- Respect all students, staff, and campus property.
- Harassment, discrimination, and vandalism are strictly prohibited.
- Disciplinary cases are handled by the Office of Student Conduct (Room 210, Admin Building).

## 6. IDs and Access
- Your Arcadia Student ID grants library, lab, gym, and dorm access.
- Lost IDs must be reported to Campus Services within 24 hours.

## 7. Health and Wellness
- The Wellness Center (Building C) offers free counseling and health checkups.
- Emergency line: ext. 911 from any campus phone.

## 8. Contact
- Dean of Students: dean.students@arcadia.edu
- Student Helpdesk: help@arcadia.edu
